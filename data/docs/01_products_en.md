# Alrouf Lighting - Product Overview (Sample)

This document is a sample knowledge-base file used for the Task 3 RAG demo.

## Streetlight Fixtures
### ALR-SL-90W
- Category: LED streetlight fixture
- Power: 90W
- Typical application: urban roads, industrial roads, parking lots
- Common requirements (project dependent): IP65+, surge protection, photometric compliance

### ALR-FL-150W
- Category: LED floodlight
- Power: 150W
- Typical application: yards, facades, stadium perimeter, industrial areas

## Outdoor Bollard Lights
### ALR-OBL-12V
- Category: outdoor bollard light
- Input: 12V (low voltage)
- Typical application: walkways, gardens, perimeter paths

## Notes
- Final specification depends on the RFQ and required standards.
- For solar solutions, panel and battery sizing depends on location and required autonomy.
