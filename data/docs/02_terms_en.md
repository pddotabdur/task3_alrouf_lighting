# Commercial Terms (Sample)

This document is a sample knowledge-base file used for the Task 3 RAG demo.

## Delivery
- Typical lead time: 3 to 6 weeks depending on stock and manufacturing schedule.
- Delivery terms in RFQs may include DAP (Delivered At Place), EXW (Ex Works), or FOB (Free On Board).

## Warranty
- Standard warranty: 2 years from delivery date (unless otherwise agreed in the quotation).
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal usage conditions.

## Payment
- For new customers, a deposit may be required.
- For repeat customers, payment terms may be negotiated (e.g., Net 30) subject to approval.

## Documentation
- The quotation can include datasheets and compliance notes if the project requires (e.g., Tarsheed).
